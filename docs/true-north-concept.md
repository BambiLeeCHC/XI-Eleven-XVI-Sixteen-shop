# True North

**Product Concept & Brand Direction**  
*Version 0.1 — Initial documentation*

## 1. Overview

True North is the community and insight layer of xixvi.shop. It is a social platform focused on community connectivity and accessible self-reflection. Users maintain profiles, share daily readings, and participate in tandem readings with friends, partners, or family. Category focuses include career, love, and money.

The experience prioritizes personal growth and higher-self orientation rather than traditional divinatory or esoteric framing.

## 2. Aesthetic Direction

- **Tone**: Enlightenment and higher-self awareness. Calm, elevated, and refined.
- **Avoid**: Heavy divinity, ornate mystical symbolism, ritualistic or overly celestial imagery.
- **Visual Language**:
  - Clean geometric forms and restrained luminous accents.
  - Soft gradients, metallic silver/graphite, cool cyan, muted indigo.
  - Contemporary, highly legible typography.
  - Minimal compositions that emphasize clarity, orientation, and quiet confidence.
- **Overall Impression**: Modern, approachable, and grounded in personal insight rather than external mystical authority.

## 3. Reimagined Zodiac Symbols

Traditional glyphs are redesigned as simplified, modern marks that remain symbolically coherent while becoming immediately accessible and suitable for digital interfaces (profiles, cards, notifications).

### Design Principles

- Essential geometry or single refined gesture.
- Consistent visual system across the twelve signs.
- Readable at small sizes.
- Subtle support for category emphasis (career / love / money) without breaking the core mark.

### Conceptual Directions

| Sign        | Concept Description |
|-------------|---------------------|
| **Aries**      | Forward-pointing chevron or ascending arc with clean apex — initiative and direction. |
| **Taurus**     | Stable horizontal base with soft upward curve — steadiness and embodied presence. |
| **Gemini**     | Two parallel verticals joined by a light bridge — duality and communication. |
| **Cancer**     | Soft open crescent or protective spiral — emotional containment and intuition. |
| **Leo**        | Radiating central point with restrained rays — presence and creative expression. |
| **Virgo**      | Precise vertical with refined loop — discernment and practical refinement. |
| **Libra**      | Balanced horizontal beam with subtle fulcrum — equilibrium and relational harmony. |
| **Scorpio**    | Focused upward arrow or controlled vertical curve — intensity and transformation. |
| **Sagittarius**| Open diagonal trajectory referencing a horizon — expansion and higher aim. |
| **Capricorn**  | Structured ascending steps or progressive form — grounded ambition. |
| **Aquarius**   | Flowing parallel waves or interconnected nodes — collective insight and exchange. |
| **Pisces**     | Two soft opposing curves that meet or dissolve — empathy and fluid connection. |

These marks may be executed as monoline icons, soft metallic dimensional forms, or minimal filled shapes depending on final system decisions. Visual concept generations have been produced for Aries through Scorpio as initial references.

## 4. Feature Integration Notes

- **User Profiles**: Zodiac mark as primary visual identifier, paired with optional current-focus tag (career / love / money / general).
- **Daily Readings**: Clean card format featuring the relevant symbol, concise insight text, and share controls.
- **Tandem Readings**: Side-by-side or overlapping symbol treatment that visually signals connection between participants while remaining elegant.
- **Category Focuses**: Lightweight secondary indicators (color accent or subtle variant) that do not overwhelm the primary symbol system.
